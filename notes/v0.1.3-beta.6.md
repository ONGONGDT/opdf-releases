## v0.1.3-beta.6

### New
- Insert Image and Paste Image: place a PNG, JPEG or WebP picture from a file, by dragging it onto the page, or from the clipboard. Remove the background, crop and rotate it, and right-click a placed picture to edit it again.
- A more compact workspace: refreshed side panels, toolbar and Properties Bar.
- 360i PDF can now become your default app for PDF files. After an install or update on a managed machine, each user gets it once, at their next Windows sign-in. If you later choose another app, it is not changed again. IT can turn this off by installing with `/NODEFAULTAPP=1`.

### Fixes
- The Pages thumbnail frame no longer lingers after "Fit page".

### Changes
- The page tile cache is larger (800 MB, was 500 MB).
