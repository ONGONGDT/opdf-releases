## v0.1.3-beta.5

### New
- Signature library: keep signatures and initials for reuse. The Sign button places your last one with a click; its menu lists Favorites and Recent. Manage Signatures lets you rename, duplicate, favorite, edit, replace the picture of, or delete each one. Saved signatures are encrypted for your Windows account.
- Initials: a second kind of signature that is placed small and keeps its proportions.
- Picture signatures: Crop & rotate lets you cut a photo down to the signature and straighten it at any angle.
- Drop PDF files from Explorer onto the Pages panel to insert them; Shift+click picks a range in Insert from PDF.
- Save As suggests "<file> - copy.pdf" in the file's own folder.

### Fixes
- The close × on a tab with unsaved changes now responds.
- Saving markup on files larger than 2 GiB works, within a memory limit checked before saving.
- "No Stroke" is kept when you create the next mark.
- Ctrl+wheel no longer changes the line width of the selected mark.
- Popups and the hover preview no longer cover the handles of the selected mark.
