## v0.1.5

### New
- **Password-protected PDFs.** Open a protected PDF with its password, work on it, and save it without losing the protection. What the owner blocked (printing, copying) stays blocked. You can also add a password to any PDF (*File > Protect PDF…*), change it, or remove it. Adding or changing a password needs a signed-in account.
- **Pages between documents.** Copy, cut and paste pages between tabs and windows with Ctrl+C / X / V, markup included, or drag them out to save as a PDF. Drop several PDFs at once and arrange them in one list, in the same order as in Explorer, choosing pages for each file. *Move to…* sends pages to a page number you type.
- **New drawing tools.** Polyline, Polygon and Polygon Cloud, drawn click by click and reshaped point by point. They are in the toolbar's Shape group with the other line and shape tools.
- **Faster markup editing.** Ctrl+drag copies a markup, even onto another page. Ctrl+V pastes at the pointer. Align and Distribute line up several markups. Copy to Pages now shows the result for each page, so you can review it or undo it.
- **A workspace set up your way.** A short setup on first launch asks how you like to work. The Select tool can either move a markup on drag or select it first, so you don't move markup by accident while panning. Pin the tools you use most to the sidebar.

### Improvements
- **Easier to move around a document.** Scrollbars on the page view, an always-visible Pages panel scrollbar, and a mouse wheel that turns pages smoothly in Document mode, like Acrobat.
- **Nicer markup.** Smoother pen strokes and lines. Text boxes grow and shrink with their text. Clearer note icons, including Acrobat's. Markup from other PDF apps displays more faithfully.
- **Shared files.** Anyone can edit anyone's markup by default, so teams can work on one file. Locked markups stay locked.
- The **Print dialog** has a cleaner look and fits small windows.

### Fixes
- **More reliable display.** Fixed flickering on some Intel graphics, and the app recovers instead of freezing when the graphics driver has a problem. If the display still misbehaves, turn on *Help > Graphics compatibility mode*.
- **Safer saving.** A full disk is reported clearly, and a save never leaves a half-written file.
- After an update, the app reopens on the page you were viewing. Text selection no longer gets stuck. Polygons closed in Acrobat stay editable.

The installer now shows the licence agreement. *Help* has the License Agreement and Third-Party Notices.
