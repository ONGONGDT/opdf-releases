## v0.1.3-beta.4

### New
- Signature: sign a PDF from Draw, Image (with background removal) or Type. Place it, move, resize, rotate, copy and delete it; it stays editable after you save and reopen.
- Bring to Front / Send to Back in the right-click menu of a selected mark. Clicking a spot where marks overlap now picks the top one; Tab cycles through the others.
- Document mode scrolls like Acrobat: the wheel moves a full page at the page edge, and Continuous mode scrolls the full distance.
- The view mode (Document or Continuous) is remembered between launches.
- The tab strip and top bar fit narrow windows.
- Merge and Find & Replace are switched off until they are finished.

### Fixes
- Saving a page with a crop and markup, accented labels, or an edit made during the save no longer loses or shifts the markup.
- Turning pages and then saving keeps page edits and markup intact.
- Windows shutdown, restart or sign-out is no longer reported as an abnormal exit.
- The optional update dialog no longer closes when you click outside it.
- Format menus of the Text Box tool open away from the toolbar edge.
- The hover preview shows only what the page does not already show.
