
## v0.1.3-beta.3

### New
- Usage data: 360PDF now sends usage and performance data (which tools are used, load times, errors and crashes) to your organization's telemetry service. It never includes file names, document contents or anything you type. A one-time "Usage data" notice explains this, and you can turn it off any time in Settings > Privacy > Send usage data.
- The installer also installs or updates the Telemetry Platform Agent, the background service that delivers this data. Uninstalling 360PDF leaves it in place, since other company apps can use it.

### Fixes
- Callout: the elbow stays square to the text box, and double-clicking the elbow resets it.
- Text Box: a border cleared to "none" stays cleared after saving and reopening.
- Shapes from other PDF tools whose appearance is an image now display as they do in those tools.
