## v0.1.2-beta.1

### Sign in
- Sign in with your work Microsoft account (single sign-on). Without signing in, the app opens documents in view-only mode.
- Marks you add are authored by your signed-in account name.
- Your session is re-checked in the background and keeps working offline for up to 15 days.

### New
- Comments panel: Conversation Navigator replaces the card list; the search box opens on demand.
- Copy to Pages: copy marks onto other pages in one undo step.
- Stamps: stamp library and picker, create text stamps and image stamps.
- Pages panel: multi-select with keyboard and rubber-band.
- App Tour and Controls & Shortcuts Quick Guide.
- One consistent set of keyboard shortcuts; zoom ladder with a true-size % label; Alt+Wheel fine zoom.
- Controls for features that are not finished yet are hidden or disabled.

### Fixes
- Text boxes, callouts and typewriter text placed on a turned page now read upright.
- Stamps keep their orientation after saving a turned page; unsaved page edits with stamps save atomically.
- A one-page document can now be selected.
- Opening a file at startup and Save As no longer fail on paths with a verbatim prefix.
- A stale sign-in token during a background re-check is treated as offline instead of an error.
