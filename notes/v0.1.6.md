## v0.1.6

### New
- **Drag a tab out into its own window.** The tab follows your pointer, lifts out of the tab strip, and the other tabs slide to close the gap. Drop it on another window's tab strip to move it there. A tab dropped outside every window is never lost.
- **Check how thoroughly Protect PDF verifies its result.** *Protect PDF* and *Manage Password* now offer a **Quick file check** (the default) and a **Full file check**. On a 250 MB, 555-page drawing set the quick check takes about 20 seconds and the full check about 170 seconds. If the quick check fails, nothing is saved and the full check is preselected for another try.

### Improvements
- **Smoother motion.** Scrolling, zooming and page turns are now timed to your monitor's refresh rate, one rhythm per monitor, so windows on different screens each stay smooth.
- **Pasting pages is more predictable.** Ctrl+V pastes after the page you clicked, else after the last ticked page, else after the page you are viewing. To add pages at the end, use *Paste Pages at End*.
- **Dragging tabs is more accurate on screens with different display scaling.** The drop position is now read at the scale of the window you are dragging over.

### Fixes
- **Dragging pages between two windows no longer freezes.** Before, dragging a thumbnail out of a window could freeze it for up to 30 seconds, a drop into another window of the app was ignored, and a second drag would not start.
- **Pages pasted from another window no longer jump to the end** of the document when you had scrolled away from the page you clicked.
- **Links inside a PDF survive page edits.** Links to other pages used to stop working after rotating, reordering, extracting or inserting pages, or after pasting pages and saving. They keep working now, and a link whose target page was not kept is removed instead of pointing to the wrong place.
