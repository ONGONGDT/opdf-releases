## v0.1.3

### New
- 360PDF is now 360i PDF. Your settings, stamps and sign-in carry over; nothing to do.
- Signature: sign a PDF from Draw, Image (with background removal) or Type. Keep signatures and initials in a library for reuse; the Sign button places your last one with a click. Crop & rotate lets you straighten a photo signature. Saved signatures are encrypted for your Windows account.
- Insert Image and Paste Image: place a PNG, JPEG or WebP picture from a file, by dragging it onto the page, or from the clipboard. Remove the background, crop and rotate it, and right-click a placed picture to edit it again.
- Printing now shows a small "Printing" window with a progress bar and a Cancel printing button, instead of leaving the Print dialog open. It closes by itself when the job is sent.
- Markup styles: each tool keeps its own Current style. "Save as Default" keeps it for next time; "Reset to Default" or "Restore Factory Style" bring it back.
- Bring to Front / Send to Back in the right-click menu of a selected mark. Clicking where marks overlap picks the top one; Tab cycles through the others.
- Drop PDF files from Explorer onto the Pages panel to insert them; Shift+click picks a range in Insert from PDF.
- Document mode scrolls like Acrobat. The view mode (Document or Continuous) is remembered between launches.
- A more compact workspace: refreshed side panels, toolbar and Properties Bar. A collapsed side panel shows its buttons as a compact block, and the page uses the full window height.
- Updating is no longer a blank wait: a small "360i PDF Update" window shows what is happening and closes by itself when the new version opens. This appears for updates installed from this version onward.
- 360i PDF can become your default app for PDF files. After an install or update on a managed machine, each user gets it once, at their next Windows sign-in. If you later choose another app, it is not changed again. IT can turn this off by installing with `/NODEFAULTAPP=1`.
- Usage data: 360i PDF sends usage and performance data (which tools are used, load times, errors and crashes) to your organization's telemetry service. It never includes file names, document contents or anything you type. A one-time notice explains this, and you can turn it off in Settings > Privacy > Send usage data. The installer also installs or updates the Telemetry Platform Agent; uninstalling 360i PDF leaves it in place.
- Save As suggests "<file> - copy.pdf" in the file's own folder.

### Fixes
- Fixed the app not opening at all on some computers with Intel graphics (for example Intel Iris Xe). The display mode is now chosen from what your graphics driver supports, and falls back safely.
- Saving no longer breaks digital signatures. Signed PDFs are never rewritten: markup is saved to a copy, and certified PDFs can be viewed or saved as an exact copy only.
- Text boxes keep their orientation and line spacing after the file is opened and saved in Adobe Acrobat.
- Saving a page with a crop and markup, accented labels, or an edit made during the save no longer loses or shifts the markup. Turning pages and then saving keeps page edits and markup intact.
- Saving markup on files larger than 2 GiB works, within a memory limit checked before saving.
- Callout: the elbow stays square to the text box, and double-clicking it resets it. Text Box: a border cleared to "none" stays cleared after saving and reopening.
- Shapes from other PDF tools whose appearance is an image now display as they do in those tools.
- Stamps, signatures and pictures no longer flicker when zoomed in very far.
- Windows shutdown, restart or sign-out is no longer reported as an abnormal exit.
- The close × on a tab with unsaved changes now responds. The optional update dialog no longer closes when you click outside it.
- "No Stroke" is kept when you create the next mark. Ctrl+wheel no longer changes the line width of the selected mark.

### Changes
- Pages become sharp faster after zooming, especially on large, detailed drawings. Switching between open documents keeps the pages already drawn.
- The page tile cache is larger (800 MB, was 500 MB).
- Merge and Find & Replace are switched off until they are finished.
- Page changes, cropping, bookmark edits and text replacement are not available on signed PDFs.
